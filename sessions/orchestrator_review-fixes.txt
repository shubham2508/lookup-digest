  **Bug found: an email can close compose's raw-excerpt block early**
  - **Where:** `digest/compose/__init__.py:60` and `:65`, with
  `digest/read/render.py:71`.
  - **Cause:** `raw_excerpts` first collapses all whitespace with
  `normalize_for_match`, which turns the excerpt into a single line, and only
  then calls `defuse(span)`. `_defuse` only breaks up lines that contain "RAW
